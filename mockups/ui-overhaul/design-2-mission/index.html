Mission Control

Agents are the heroes, the machine is secondary. Every surface opens on what the coding agents are doing right now: who is working, on which repo, for how long, what they served and what they told you. Machine state (power, RAM, reprovision) sits one column to the left, and the rare destructive work lives behind its own tab. The Matrix heritage stays, but disciplined: near-black green grounds, a phosphor tier scale, and a bright green that appears only on things that are live.
